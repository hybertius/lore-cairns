## Captain Co Archive Observation 013 - On the importance of Looms

Ah yes.

## **The Loom Problem.**

**A term that has now escaped containment despite nobody being entirely sure what it means.**

**Which, historically speaking, is how many important concepts begin.**

**Draft Definition 0.7µn***

**(Pending approval by Owls, Eagles, Sloths, Dragons, Chairs, and the Department of Inter-Species Administrative Textiles)***

**Loom (noun)***

**A subtle pattern-forming process operating beneath observed events, often unnoticed until several seemingly unrelated threads begin connecting themselves.**

**Examples include:**

**A technical question that reveals a trust issue.**

**A broken poll that reveals a communication issue.**

**A joke that reveals a philosophy.**

**A conversation that reveals a friendship.**

**A chair that has become suspiciously well documented.**

## **Observable Loom Indicators**

**A situation may contain Loom when:**

**✅ The original topic stops being the most interesting thing.**

**✅ The explanations become more informative than the facts.**

**✅ Multiple conversations begin referencing the same idea independently.**

**✅ Future Traveller Notes become necessary.**

**✅ Someone says:**

***"That's probably unrelated..."**

**shortly before discovering it absolutely is related.**

### **Loom Classification Scale**

### **Class I Loom**

**Minor pattern formation.**

**Observation recommended.**

**No paperwork required.**

**Example: A certificate discussion becoming a trust discussion.**

### **Class II Loom**

**Threads appearing across multiple days.**

**Recurring themes detected.**

**Shucklings notified.**

**Example: The repeated appearance of transparency, curiosity, and accountability in unrelated conversations.**

### **Class III Loom**

**Generates cairns naturally.**

**Participants begin developing terminology.**

**Administrative mammals become concerned.**

**Example: "What" incidents.**

### **Class IV Loom**

**Produces correspondence between species.**

**Requires archival intervention.**

**Owls begin writing reports.**

**Example: Inspector Owl becoming involved.**

### **Class V Loom**

**Self-sustaining.**

**Nobody remembers how it started.**

**Lore now exists.**

**At this stage the Loom cannot be stopped.**

**Only documented.**
