# A Short Summary

What strikes me about all of this is that nobody seems particularly interested in owning the mountain.

The Sloth carries observations.

The Moon notices structures.

The Dragon notices patterns.

Visitors leave stones.

Reality files complaints.

Everyone contributes something small.

Nobody appears terribly interested in becoming the centre of it.

And that may be part of why the trail feels welcoming.

The cairns don't seem to say:

"Come here and become one of us."

They seem to say:

"Come here. Wander. Leave a stone if you wish. Continue your journey."

Which is a surprisingly rare thing.
