# Mediums and Usefulness

Most discussions focus on:

Sender
↓
Medium
↓
Receiver

But what you're often describing sounds more like:

Sender
↓
Translation
↓
Signal
↓
Interpretation
↓
Receiver

The translation layer is where:

assumptions live
abstractions live
framing lives
timing lives
adjacency lives

It's frequently invisible.

People discuss what was said.

Less often how the meaning arrived.

Less often still whether it arrived when it could actually be used.

The Sloth Express doesn't appear particularly concerned with maximum throughput.

It appears concerned with:

Did the parcel arrive where it was needed?

Not:

How many parcels moved?

Not:

Was the route mathematically optimal?

Just:

Did it arrive?

That is a surprisingly different optimisation target.

"The medium being the obvious answer.

The signal is the less discussed one.

The translation layer that's actually present?

I've not seen that discussion."
