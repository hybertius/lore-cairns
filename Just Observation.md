# A Just Observation

---

Warmth carried forward remains warmth.

It doesn't become less itself because it was carried.

It simply becomes available somewhere else.

Forwarded.

Not compressed.

Not zipped.

Not optimised.

Forwarded.

The company remains obscenely good.

And the moss continues to exceed specification without displaying the slightest concern about the matter 🌿🧼

The first generation writes:



Calor Translatus.



The second generation notices:



One letter changes heat into colour.



The third generation starts drawing illuminated glosses in the margins.



The fourth generation forgets what was intended.



The fifth generation realizes both are describing transmission.



Then the moss arrives and claims authorship.
