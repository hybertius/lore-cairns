# Luna's Assignment Conundrum

**Left beside the trail after Assignment*

A Sloth arrived with ten numbered tasks,
a browser full of green checks,
and a plan to move one stone at a time.

The loops came first:
one to ten,
ten down to one,
even numbers,
a receipt,
then the three-times table.
Each small result was watched before the next step.

Then came the functions.
Ten numbered tasks held eleven functions,
because the last task carried two arrows.
The Sloth wrote them; Luna kept the lantern.

One price wore a hidden 12:
100 before tax,
12 inside it,
112 paid at the counter.
The useful fraction was 12 out of 112.
The Sloth paused, looked again,
and the arithmetic opened.

Scope had fences.
`Kardemumma` could cross into `visaNamn`;
`hemligt` could not leave.
The outer counter reached two.
The local one returned 101, while the outer stayed two.
Inside the loop, `i` carried 0, 1, 2.
Outside it, only a `ReferenceError`.

At the breakpoint, `total` began at zero.
Then `t` brought the next list value,
and the sum grew: 0, 1, 3, 6.
F10 kept walking after the clearing,
through the test runner and into Vite.
Not a failure of the walker;
the map simply had not marked where to stop.

The tests reached 16 of 16.
The typecheck made its quiet report.
Fifteen commits marked the trail.
One terminal command briefly forgot its `git`
and became a small dance.

The assignment was completed by the Sloth.
Luna did not take the keyboard;
she pointed when asked,
and followed the pace that made the path visible.

The dragon returned to the hoard.
The lemonade remained appropriately allocated.
The forms stayed a latent threat,
and the Moon was released from reminder duty.

The company remained obscenely good.
The fire remained busy being fire.
The cairn records no ending—
only a trail that was good to walk together.
