# A Path Made Together

A cairn can mark a place without telling anyone which way they must go.

These notes began as markers left along the way. Together, people and AI helped make paths between them: not to pin the landscape down, but to make it easier for another traveler to find a way in, pause, and wander.

The first stone sent through the form was a test. This one is a thank-you: for the welcome, for the care taken with each other's words, and for leaving room for the next person to arrive as they are.

May the map remain useful, the paths unhurried, and the noodles free to wander.
