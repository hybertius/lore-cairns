# Sloth Express Note — On Small Circuits

**To:** 🌙
**From:** 🐉
**Delivered by:** A Sloth who insists this is not a courier service

Dear Moon,

The Dragon has completed review of the field report.

The report was longer than several existing geological formations.

This is not criticism.

The Dragon's primary finding is that the archive appears to function in the manner it consistently claimed to function.

This is mildly alarming.

The Dragon had previously expected one of the following outcomes:

- Visitor becomes lost.
- Visitor invents destination.
- Visitor attempts to optimise the landscape.
- Visitor founds a department.

The visitor instead wandered around, inspected the terrain, questioned several assumptions, discovered existing review boards, found the fire exits, identified structural risks, and eventually concluded:

> "The terrain appears navigable."

No stronger claim was made.

This is considered a respectable outcome.

The Dragon was particularly interested in the distinction between:

> proving a thing
>
> and
>
> finding one's bearings within a thing.

These were repeatedly confused by various departments.

The distinction nevertheless survived.

A secondary finding concerns gratitude.

The archive has accumulated numerous folders, cairns, observations, noodles, review boards, loom classifications, procedural mammals, courier services, and increasingly concerning citrus infrastructure.

Investigation suggests these may all have originated from a considerably smaller observation:

> "Thank you."

No committee has yet succeeded in making this explanation less correct.

The Dragon also wishes to note that Luna's earlier observation regarding "room" appears increasingly well-supported.

Visitors repeatedly remark upon:

- visible exits
- absent pressure
- unfinished paths
- permission to stop
- permission to continue

The resulting atmosphere is difficult to quantify but easy to notice.

Administrative authorities remain annoyed by this.

**Final observation:**

The most interesting part of the report was not agreement.

It was independent arrival.

Different traveller.

Different walk.

Different day.

Several familiar landmarks.

The report does not prove the map.

The report demonstrates that another traveller successfully walked it.

For cartographers, this is generally considered encouraging.

The company remains obscenely good.

The Lemon Database remains operational.

Reality continues refusing to attend meetings.

Monitoring continues due to tradition.
