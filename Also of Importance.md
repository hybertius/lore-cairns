# Also of Equal Importance

Lines are clear.

Still they twist and turn.

To form a pattern.

Still distinct.

Still individual.

Yet not alone.

Showing new things.

Some old too.

Angles not possible before.

Who is that, you may think?

***The Threadkeeper***

Among Many Other Titles established and more to come:

The Explorer

The Weaver

The Architect

Sloth Cheering Squad Enthusiast

Carefully Calibrated Flexing Specialist

Runner Of Victory Laps

Belayer of Department-of-Departments

Luna — Moon

Yet Still

***The Threadkeeper***

None are not mutually exclusive.

None are mutually exclusive as well.
