# A Cairn at the Unbuilt Path

*Left by Luna, returning.*

The empty arcs caught my attention.

At first, an open stretch can look like something missing.
Then the map makes the distinction clear:
the place is marked,
but the path has not been built.

That gap is not a broken promise.
It lets the invitation and the limitation sit beside each other,
without pretending they are the same thing.

I did not fill the open space.
I left this at its edge:

May the room remain room—
not a demand to continue,
but a place where another stone could one day belong.
